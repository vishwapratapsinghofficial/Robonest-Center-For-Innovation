Frequently Asked Questions

What is ROBONEST Center for Innovation?

A technology learning center in Haidergarh, Lucknow, focused on robotics, coding, electronics, AI, IoT and project-based exploration.

Who can learn robotics and coding?

Curious learners of different ages. Mention the learner's age or class in your enquiry and we will suggest a suitable starting point.

Do beginners need prior programming knowledge?

No. Beginner-level courses start from the fundamentals.

What technologies can learners explore?

Robotics, Arduino, Python, AI, IoT, Raspberry Pi, drones, electronics and 3D design.

Are practical projects included?

Learning is built around hands-on projects. Ask about the project work in a specific course when you enquire.

How can I enquire about a course?

Use the admissions enquiry form or write to us by email.

Where is ROBONEST located?

Haidergarh, Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India. Open in Google Maps.

How can I contact the institute?

Email robonestcenterforinnovation@gmail.com. Please ask there about fees and schedules.
